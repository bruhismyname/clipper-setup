# TikTok Format Signals

Platform dynamics on TikTok differ from long-form video or other short-form surfaces. This reference documents key behavioral factors to evaluate when scoring candidates.

## Platform Evaluation Metrics

Distribution depends primarily on watch time, completion rate, and rewatch behavior:

1. **Completion Over Total Length**: A concise clip watched to completion outperforms an extended clip abandoned midway.
2. **First Three Seconds**: Viewers decide to stay or scroll within the opening seconds.
3. **Rewatches**: Seamless loop transitions where the ending flows naturally into the opening phrase encourage repeat views.

## Duration Categories

| Category | Typical Use |
|---|---|
| **8-15s** | High completion, concise punchline or single reaction. |
| **15-45s** | Standard narrative window with room for premise setup and resolution. |
| **45-90s** | Detailed story or complex explanation. Standard requirement for monetization programs (e.g. Creator Rewards). |
| **90s+** | Generally inefficient for standalone short-form clips. Should be subdivided. |

## High-Performing Signal Types

| Signal | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Spontaneous reactions** | Authentic emotional tone that anchors the dialog |
| **Quick reversals** | Premise and counter-intuitive resolution within 10 seconds |
| **Concrete metrics** | Specific verifiable numbers ("$40,000", "1,400 times") |
| **Debated perspectives** | Direct points of view that invite discussion |
| **Direct address** | Clear contextual relevance to the viewer |
| **Clear conclusions** | Conclusive resolution of the opening question |

## Low-Performing Signal Types

| Signal | Failure Mode |
|---|---|
| **Context traps** | Phrases like "as stated earlier" that require outside context |
| **Extended preambles** | Excessive background information before establishing the premise |
| **Unanchored credentials** | Generic resumes without concrete outcomes |
| **Internal references** | Slang or inside jokes understandable only to longtime subscribers |
| **Unformatted lists** | Dry recitations without narrative stakes |
| **Excessive terminology** | Highly specialized jargon with narrow relevance |

## The Re-Cut Strategy

When a retention peak features a weak opening phrase, do not discard the segment. Re-cut the clip starting at the most compelling statement inside the peak window, allowing supporting context to follow.

## Operational Caveat

Individual video performance depends on variables including publishing timing, account history, sound selection, and algorithmic distribution. These guidelines improve baseline probabilities rather than guaranteeing specific view thresholds.
